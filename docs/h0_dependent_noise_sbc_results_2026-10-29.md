## Locked model

A host at redshift z has mean measured distance μ(z,H0)=cz/H0 and standard deviation σ(z,H0)=sqrt(8²+[0.12 μ(z,H0)]²) Mpc. The population has 12 discrete redshifts on [0.025, 0.16], with positive linearly increasing weights. Each dataset contains three detected events. Events are generated by sampling a host, drawing from the same H0-dependent Gaussian noise model, and retaining distances d≥300 Mpc. The likelihood marginalizes over the discrete host mixture and conditions on the fixed number of detections using log L_selected(H0)=Σ log L_i(H0)−3 log β(H0), where β(H0) is computed from the same heteroscedastic noise law. Inference uses a uniform prior on [50, 90] km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹ and 161 grid points including both endpoints.

The four truth regimes were a uniform prior draw and fixed values H0=60, 70, 80. Seed ranges were disjoint by regime. The raw table stores each replicate’s seed, true value, and detected-distance vector, allowing the independent auditor to replay stochastic data generation.

For the prior-drawn regime, the ten rank-decile counts were [18, 31, 36, 30, 27, 35, 33, 34, 31, 25]. A descriptive ten-bin chi-square statistic was 8.87 on 9 degrees of freedom (p=0.450); mean rank was 0.514. This is compatible with internal prior-predictive calibration at the resolution of this experiment. Empirical 68% and 90% coverage are mildly above nominal, with uncertainty represented by Wilson intervals.

The fixed-truth simulations show conditional shrinkage toward the center of the uniform prior: the posterior mean tends to overestimate H0=60 and underestimate H0=80, while fixed H0=70 bias is smaller. That is a meaningful feature of this low-information, three-event toy inference, not proof of a software defect. Ranks at a fixed parameter value are not expected to be uniform in the same way as ranks aggregated over a prior-predictive draw; fixed-truth rank histograms are descriptive diagnostics here, not the same criterion as prior-predictive SBC.

## Interpretation

**Passed:** the stochastic generator and independently coded calculation reproduce every saved record and posterior summary to floating-point precision; prior-predictive ranks are broadly compatible with uniform ranks for this sample; saved artifact hashes match.

**Unknown:** whether this likelihood is a useful approximation to realistic gravitational-wave data; how results change with event count, host-population shape, uncertain redshifts, rate marginalization or detector response; and whether the separate host-catalogue incompleteness correction is stable under more realistic conditions.

This test makes H0-dependent noise and selection normalization explicit and falsifiable. This module is a separate toy prototype: it was not used to generate the earlier locked 500-replicate selection-misspecification Stage B result, and the two studies must not be conflated.
