## Interpretation

The curvature-ordered posterior-median bias pattern persists under this independent seed schedule: the mean bias rises from approximately +4.1–4.4 at gamma=-1 to +10.6–11.3 at gamma=+1. The paired gamma=+1 minus gamma=-1 difference is about +6.7 to +7.2 km s^-1 Mpc^-1. Increasing validation size from 100 to 500 does not materially reduce mean median bias in these six cells.

Coverage is below nominal in all six cells, particularly at the 68% level. This finding is conditional on the fixed-truth four-event design and must be interpreted alongside the substantial positive bias already present at gamma=0. The observed pattern is not proof that inclusion misspecification alone causes the bias. The five quadrature NON_EVALUABLE cells are retained in the denominator; success-only coverage is accompanied by all-attempt bounds above.

## Limitations and next work

- This is a fixed-truth repeated-sampling pilot, not joint simulation-based calibration (SBC).
- The experiment estimates the degree-one inclusion model under quadratic-logit inclusion; it does not establish universal estimator superiority.
- It is synthetic low-redshift Euclidean work, not an observational H0 measurement, real survey validation, or LVK/GWTC analysis.
- The H0 grid has not yet been independently refined on this new seed-2026111930 artifact. Coefficient quadrature convergence does not establish H0-grid convergence.
- The 100 replicates per cell still leave substantial Monte Carlo uncertainty in coverage.
- The correctly specified gamma=0 cell is positively biased, so prior/likelihood/event-count effects remain an important competing explanation.
- Next: independently reconstruct this artifact's key summaries from raw cell outputs; correct the stale auditor interpretation string with a focused regression test; run same-seed H0-grid refinement on a preregistered subset; then diagnose the gamma=0 baseline and plan joint SBC with truth draws from the stated generative prior.
